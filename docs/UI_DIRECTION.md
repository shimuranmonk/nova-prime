# Nova Ultimate Interface Direction

## Approved visual hierarchy

Nova Ultimate uses Nova Trainer as its primary interaction model: a compact application header, prominent connection and calibration status, card-based drill libraries, custom-drill groups, and training totals. It incorporates Nova Plus controls where they improve the training workflow: difficulty levels, Reps/Time/Music/MSYNC mode selection, count or duration, pause, and compact session actions.

## Implemented app shell

- Training is the default route; the development-style landing hero is removed.
- Connection and calibration appear first, but remain disabled until their safety milestones are complete.
- Reps and Time controls operate the existing local dry-run controller.
- Music and MSYNC are visible as disabled future modes rather than represented as complete.
- The built-in drill area contains the verified 34-drill Nova Trainer catalogue with its 6 Basic, 12 Combined, and 16 Complex entries.
- Custom groups A, B, and C are visible, while New Drill remains disabled until the editor is implemented.
- Data, About, Diagnostics, and Roadmap are moved into an application drawer.
- Active-session progress and Stop remain prominent and accessible.
- The layout is responsive for phone, tablet, and web use from the same static bundle.

## Visual language

The base presentation is light, compact, and card-led, with Nova teal for selected and primary actions. Nova Plus contributes the segmented modes and compact parameter controls without replacing the Trainer-oriented information hierarchy. Status is communicated through text as well as color.

## Safety representation

The interface never presents disabled robot, calibration, editor, Music, or MSYNC functionality as operational. Simulation sessions remain explicitly labelled and continue to record zero physically dispatched balls.
