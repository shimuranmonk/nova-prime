# JewelFlow record

Nova Prime was defined through the following approved stages:

1. **Business Challenge** — Nova Plus and Nova Trainer split important capabilities across incompatible implementations.
2. **Intent** — Create one local-first product using Trainer as the robot foundation and Plus for unique Music, MSYNC, statistics, sharing, and later voice capabilities.
3. **Intent Analysis** — Resolve drill, session, storage, MSYNC timing, safety, migration, and device conflicts before requirements.
4. **Product Requirements Document** — Define phased functional and non-functional requirements.
5. **Solution Specification** — Define the canonical architecture, schemas, interfaces, migration, verification, deployment, and rollback.
6. **Generated Solution** — `main` contains Milestone 0, the canonical-domain foundation, local sessions, the hybrid app shell, and the authoritative 34-drill Nova Trainer catalogue. The custom-drill increment adds persistent Groups A/B/C management, atomic import/export, selection, and simulated sessions. The visual-editor increment adds draft-based Levels 1–3 authoring. The guided-calibration increment adds Standard and Complete manual observation plans, local profiles, Quick Check, and calibrated visual estimates. The 0.2 Music increment adds deduplicated local tracks, playlists, Quick Music, unified audio sessions, and playable-audio recovery. The 0.3 MSYNC increment adds frozen-v1 parsing, strict validation, exact audio attachment, pre-start revalidation, and synchronized cue simulation with Robot Lead planning. The 0.4 Insights increment adds device-local activity summaries and deterministic, explainable plan guidance. The 0.5 sharing increment adds explicit versioned custom-drill packs, optional device sharing, validated preview, and confirmed atomic import. None crosses the live-robot safety boundary.
